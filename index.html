How it works

From first message to full recovery

A clear path in five steps — and you're never guessing what comes next.

01

First contact

Reach us on WhatsApp or through the form on this site — whichever is easier. Send what you already have: a diagnosis, test results, even a phone photo of a report. No need to prepare anything formal first.

02

Medical review

Our medical team reviews your case and matches it with the right clinic and specialist from our partner network — the decision is based on your condition, not convenience.

03

Clear plan, clear price

You receive a proposed treatment plan and cost in writing before you decide anything. No hidden steps, no surprises later.

04

Travel & arrival

Once you're ready to proceed, we handle the visa invitation, transfers and accommodation — so the only thing you need to do is arrive.

05

Treatment & beyond

During treatment, you're accompanied and everything is translated for you. After you return home, we stay reachable for follow-up questions and support.
